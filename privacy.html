Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Goaldyn is a personal planner for tasks, habits, goals, and focus time. It was designed to be private by default. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information. It covers the Goaldyn app on iPhone and on Android; where the two work differently, it says so.

The short version

Goaldyn does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. The app has no user accounts and no sign-in, does no tracking, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. Goaldyn makes no network requests of its own, and the Android app does not even hold the permission to use the internet. Everything you create stays on your device unless something you turned on or did yourself makes a copy. That can be your phone's own backup or transfer to a new phone, events added to a calendar your phone syncs with an account, a text copy you share, or a backup file you save where Goaldyn offers one. Each is explained below.

What stays on your device

Your tasks, events, lists, habits, goals, focus sessions, and settings are stored only on your phone, in a local database and settings files in the app's own private storage, which other apps on your phone cannot open. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else; Goaldyn has no server of its own to send it to. The only copies Goaldyn makes are the ones you ask for: the events it adds to your calendar if you turn on calendar sync (see Calendar below), and a text copy you share or a backup file you save, where Goaldyn offers that (see Backup files below).

Your phone's own backup. If you have turned on your phone's backup, or you move to a new phone with its transfer tool, your phone's system may include Goaldyn's data, as it does for other apps. That copy is made by your phone's system, not by Goaldyn, and is kept where your phone keeps its backups, usually in your own account. We cannot see it.

Widgets. A Goaldyn widget on your home screen shows a little of what is already in the app, such as today's tasks; the Support page lists the widgets each phone has. On iPhone, a running focus session also shows its countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. These read the same on-device data, and nothing is sent anywhere to fill them.

Links you open. When you tap a link in Goaldyn, such as this policy, the Support page, or a link on an event, Goaldyn hands it to your phone's browser or to another app on your phone, and that app opens it. In the Android app you can also tap the place of an event from your other calendars, and Goaldyn hands that text to your phone's maps app in the same way. Goaldyn itself does not go online.

Calendar (optional)

Goaldyn asks for calendar access only when you tap Allow on the Calendar tab or turn on a calendar option in Settings > Calendar. The app works fully without it.

Showing your calendars. Once calendar access is allowed, "Show my other calendars" is on until you turn it off, even if you allowed access only to use sync. Goaldyn then reads the events in the calendars already on your phone and shows them next to your plans, on the Calendar tab and in the Schedule on the Today tab. For each event it reads the title, time, location, link, notes, whether it repeats, who organized it, and the calendar and account it belongs to. This is read-only: Goaldyn does not change these events. They are read on your device when they are shown and are not saved in Goaldyn unless you tap "Add to Goaldyn" on an event. You choose which calendars appear, or turn this off, in Settings > Calendar.

Sync with your calendar. If you turn this on, Goaldyn adds your Goaldyn events to the calendar you pick: a default calendar until you choose another, or a new "Goaldyn" calendar if you ask for one. It adds your open tasks too, if they have a date or a reminder (with or without a time), unless you turn that part off. It also reads back the edits you make to them in your phone's calendar app (for example moving or renaming one) so both stay in step. Goaldyn changes or deletes only the events it added or the events you added to Goaldyn; it leaves the rest of your calendar alone.

While sync is on, the "Add events to" row in Settings > Calendar shows which calendar is in use. On iPhone the default is your phone's default calendar. Android has no single default calendar, so there Goaldyn uses an account's main calendar; with several accounts, it takes the first in alphabetical order that has a main calendar it can add to. If no account has one, it uses another account calendar it can add to, or else a calendar kept only on the phone. A new "Goaldyn" calendar made on Android stays on the phone. On iPhone it is made in iCloud when the phone has an iCloud calendar account; otherwise in the account your phone's default calendar belongs to, or on the phone itself if no account takes it.

All of this happens on your device through your phone's own calendar storage (on iPhone, Apple's EventKit framework). Goaldyn never signs in to a calendar account, and your calendar data never leaves your device through Goaldyn: it is never sent to us or to anyone else. If the calendar you pick belongs to an account your phone already syncs (for example iCloud on an iPhone), your phone keeps the events in it in sync with that account, as it does for any calendar event.

Voice task entry (optional)

If you use the microphone button to dictate a task, your phone asks you to allow the microphone (and, on iPhone, speech recognition). Goaldyn then uses your phone's own speech recognition to turn what you say into text: Apple's on iPhone, and on Android the speech service your phone is set up with. On phones that support it for your language, recognition happens on the device. Where it cannot, Goaldyn tells you so and goes on only if you agree. On iPhone it asks once and remembers your answer; on Android it asks again whenever the app has been restarted. Whenever recognition is not on the device, the screen says so while you speak, and the audio may be processed on the servers of your phone's speech service, under that provider's privacy policy (Apple's on iPhone). Goaldyn itself never records, stores, or transmits your audio; only the resulting text appears in your task.

The Search screen in the Android app has a microphone button that works differently: it opens your phone's own voice input screen. That screen belongs to your phone's voice input app, which does the listening under its own privacy policy and gives Goaldyn only the text.

On iPhone you can also add a task or a habit by voice, or from a shortcut, through the assistant built into your phone. That assistant is Apple's, and Apple handles what you say under its own privacy policy; Goaldyn receives only the text.

Notifications and alarms

If you grant notification permission, Goaldyn schedules local reminders on your device, along with its other alerts, such as habit check-ins (on once you have a habit, until you switch them off in Settings) and the alert at the end of a focus session. These are created on your phone and handled entirely by your phone's system. Nothing is sent to your phone from outside to make them appear, and they are not transmitted anywhere.

Recent versions of Android have a second setting called "Alarms & reminders". If you allow it for Goaldyn, your phone delivers reminders and the alert at the end of a focus session at the minute you set, and "Ring as alarm" can be turned on; without it the phone decides when, which can be late. It is a timing setting only and gives Goaldyn no access to your information. On Android, a "Ring as alarm" reminder rings and vibrates until you stop it, for five minutes at most. Goaldyn does this on your phone, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Backup files (optional)

Where Goaldyn's Settings has "Save backup file" (the Android app does), Goaldyn makes a backup file only when you tap it. The file is saved through your phone's own file picker to the place you choose. Goaldyn needs no storage permission, no account, and no network connection for this, and the file is never sent to us. If you choose a folder that belongs to a cloud storage app, that app handles the file from there, under its own privacy policy.

A backup file is plain, readable text and is not encrypted. It holds what is in Goaldyn: your tasks, events, habits and their check-in history, goals, checklists, notes, lists, focus sessions, items in Trash, and Goaldyn's own log of your activity and of the notifications it showed you. It can also hold a few settings, such as your theme. Anyone who has the file can read what you wrote, so keep it somewhere you trust. "Restore from backup file" reads a file you pick on your phone and, after you confirm, replaces what is in Goaldyn on that phone with it. If calendar sync is on, the events Goaldyn added to your calendar for items that are not in the backup are then removed from that calendar.

Text copies you share. When you share a text copy from Goaldyn, with a Share option or with "Export as text" where Settings has it, Goaldyn hands a readable text copy to your phone's share sheet, and the copy goes only where you then send it. On Android you can also share text from another app into Goaldyn; Goaldyn receives only that text and saves it as a task only when you confirm.

No data collected

Consistent with the above, we collect no data from Goaldyn on any phone. Goaldyn's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected." Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for you to request, correct, or delete on our side — deleting the app removes its local data from your device. Events Goaldyn added to your calendar stay in that calendar; to remove them, use Settings > Calendar > Remove Goaldyn events from calendar before deleting the app. Backup files and text copies you saved or shared stay where you put them until you delete them. A copy in your phone's own backup may remain after you delete the app; you manage it in your phone's backup settings. While it exists, your phone may bring that data back, for example when you set up a phone from that backup or, on Android, when you install Goaldyn again.

Children

Goaldyn does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Goaldyn is not directed to children under 13.
