Goaldyn Support

Goaldyn is a private planner for tasks, habits, goals, and deep-focus time — all on your phone.

Get help

Email info@nrdyn.com and we'll get back to you. Please include your phone model, its system version, the Goaldyn version shown under About in Settings, and a short description of what you were doing.

Common questions

Where is my data? Everything lives on your phone. Goaldyn has no account and sends nothing anywhere itself, so your tasks and habits stay private to your device. The exceptions are your choice. If you turn on calendar sync, Goaldyn adds your events, and your tasks that have a date or a reminder unless you turn that part off, to the calendar you pick. If that calendar belongs to an account your phone syncs, your phone syncs them with it. If you share a text copy, or save a backup file where Goaldyn's Settings offers one, it goes where you put it. And if your phone's own backup is on, your phone may include Goaldyn's data in it.

How do I add a task quickly? On the Tasks tab, type into the "Add a task" field on iPhone, or tap + and type on Android — phrases like "tomorrow at 9am" or "today at 5pm" set the date and time automatically. Tap the microphone to dictate instead.

What do the colors on due dates mean? In the default theme, red is overdue, orange is due today, gold is due tomorrow, and grey is later — so the next thing that needs you is always obvious. Other themes keep the same four steps in their own colors.

How does calendar sync work? In Settings > Calendar, turn on "Sync with your calendar", allow calendar access, and pick a calendar under "Add events to". Your Goaldyn events appear in that calendar. So do your open tasks that have a date or a reminder, with or without a time, if you leave "Include tasks with a time" on. Move or edit one in your phone's calendar app and Goaldyn follows. If you delete a synced event in the calendar app, Goaldyn moves that event to its Trash, where you can restore it. If you delete a synced task there, the task just loses its date; the task itself is never deleted. To take Goaldyn's events back out of your calendar, use "Remove Goaldyn events from calendar" in Settings > Calendar; everything in Goaldyn stays.

Can I see my other calendars in Goaldyn? Yes. Tap Allow on the Calendar tab. Events from the calendars already on your phone then appear next to your plans, on the Calendar tab and in the Schedule on the Today tab, each in its calendar's color. Goaldyn only reads them: tap one for its details and, where the event allows it, "Add to Goaldyn". Choose which calendars show, or turn this off, in Settings > Calendar.

How do habits and streaks work? Check a habit on each day it's scheduled; Goaldyn keeps a per-day log and shows your streak and consistency. Open a habit to see its numbers over the last 7, 30, or 90 days, or all time. On iPhone you can also choose the window the Progress tab uses, from the same four, in Settings.

What is the focus timer? A Pomodoro-style timer with breaks (short and long breaks on iPhone). While it runs, a live countdown shows outside the app: in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen on iPhone, and in a notification on Android. You can link a session to a task (on iPhone, to a habit as well) so your focus time adds up in the statistics.

Can I create an event or a task from the calendar? Yes — tap an empty time slot in the day, 3-day, or week view, or use the + button. A new event opens with its start and end filled in; use the Event | Task switch at the top if you want a task instead.

Why did a reminder arrive late, or not at all? Your phone delivers Goaldyn's reminders, so its settings decide whether they reach you on time. Notifications must be allowed for Goaldyn, or nothing can appear. If your phone has an "Alarms & reminders" setting, it must be allowed for Goaldyn, or a reminder can be up to an hour late. And if your phone can restrict an app's battery use, it must not be restricting Goaldyn's, or it may hold reminders back. The next three answers take them one at a time; if your phone has only the first setting, only that answer applies to you. Where Goaldyn's Settings has a "Reminder health" section, it shows the state of each one, and tapping a row takes you to where you change it. If a row opens nothing, change that setting for Goaldyn in your phone's own Settings app.

How do I turn notifications on? Goaldyn can notify you only if you allow it. If you said no when your phone asked, or turned notifications off later, open Settings in Goaldyn: it shows whether notifications are on, under Notifications or Reminder health. Use the button or row there to allow them, or to open your phone's settings for Goaldyn and turn them on. You can also open your phone's own Settings app, find Goaldyn, and turn notifications on there. While they are off, no reminder, habit check-in, or timer alert can appear.

How do I allow alarms and reminders? Some phones have a separate "Alarms & reminders" setting that lets an app deliver a reminder at the exact minute. If yours has it and it is off for Goaldyn, Goaldyn asks the first time you set a reminder: tap Allow in the "Get reminders on time" sheet, turn the switch on for Goaldyn on the settings screen that opens, and come back. You can do the same at any time from the "Alarms & reminders" row under Reminder health in Goaldyn's Settings. Until it is allowed, reminders and the alert at the end of a focus session may be late, and "Ring as alarm" stays off. If your phone has no such setting, there is nothing to do.

Could my phone's battery settings be making reminders late? On some phones, yes. Some phones limit what an app may do in the background to save battery. If Goaldyn's battery use is restricted, your phone may hold its reminders back. Where Goaldyn's Settings has Reminder health, the Battery row says "Restricted" or "Not restricted"; tap it to open Goaldyn's page in your phone's settings and remove the restriction there. Goaldyn never asks for an exemption from battery saving by itself; that choice stays with you. If your phone has no battery setting for individual apps, there is nothing to do.

How do I back up my data or move it to another phone? On any phone, your phone's own backup or transfer tool may carry Goaldyn's data to a new phone along with your other apps. Where Goaldyn's Settings has "Save backup file" (the Android app does), you can also keep a copy yourself: tap it and choose where to keep the file. It is named goaldyn-backup followed by the date, and it holds what you keep in Goaldyn. To bring it back, or to move to another phone where Goaldyn's Settings has "Restore from backup file", get the file onto that phone, open Settings in Goaldyn there, tap "Restore from backup file", and pick it. Goaldyn checks the file, shows when it was saved and what it holds, and asks before it replaces anything. Restoring replaces everything in Goaldyn on that phone. If calendar sync is on, events Goaldyn added to your calendar for items that are not in the backup are removed from that calendar. Calendar connections, sounds, "Ring as alarm" choices, and habit check-in times belong to each phone and are not part of a backup file. Goaldyn makes a backup file only when you ask and never sends it anywhere. The file is plain readable text with no password, so keep it somewhere private. Where Settings has "Export as text", that is different: it shares a readable list, which cannot be restored.

How do I add a Goaldyn widget? Touch and hold an empty part of your home screen, choose the widgets option your phone shows, and find Goaldyn. The widgets depend on your phone. On iPhone: Today (today's tasks and how many you've done) and Focus (today's focus time). On Android: Today Plan (today's tasks, which you can check off without opening the app), Quick Add (one tap to open Goaldyn and add something new), and Habit Streak (the streak of your first habit, with this week at a glance). A widget that asks you to open Goaldyn fills in once you have opened the app.

How do I remove the app and its data? Delete Goaldyn from your phone like any app; that removes everything Goaldyn stored on it. Events Goaldyn added to your calendar stay in that calendar. To remove them, use Settings > Calendar > Remove Goaldyn events from calendar before you delete the app. Backup files you saved stay where you saved them until you delete them. If your phone's own backup is on, a copy may remain there, and your phone may bring it back, for example when you set up a phone from that backup or, on Android, when you install Goaldyn again. You manage that copy in your phone's backup settings.
