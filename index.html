Private by design

Your plans are nobody's business.

Goaldyn's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected" — and that's the whole architecture, not a setting.

  • No account and no sign-in — the app works the moment it's installed.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • Everything is stored on your device. Calendar and voice features use Apple's own on-device frameworks with your permission.
  • Delete the app and its data goes with it. Read the full policy.
